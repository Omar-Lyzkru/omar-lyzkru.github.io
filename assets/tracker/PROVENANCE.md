## Origin

Both are **screenshots of the live app** at <https://omar-lyzkru.github.io/tiny-task-tracker/> (source
[`Omar-Lyzkru/tiny-task-tracker`](https://github.com/Omar-Lyzkru/tiny-task-tracker) at `bc45502`), captured on
October 19, 2026 with Playwright Chromium (1280 × 800 viewport, device scale factor 1.5), then resized and encoded as WebP.

The browser profile was fresh and isolated, so no personal tasks appear. Four sample tasks were typed in for the
capture, and two were marked complete. The page was then reloaded, and the first screenshot shows the same four tasks restored from
`localStorage`. The second screenshot shows the Active filter with the first task in inline-edit mode. Nothing was
edited after capture apart from resizing.
